# Mackenzie O'Brien

AI safety researcher and builder at Mac Labs AI.

The research lab is [Mac Labs AI](https://github.com/Mac-Labs-AI).

I work on interpretability, unlearning, abstention and epistemics, world models (JEPA-style), and how models behave toward users. A longer list of projects is on my [portfolio](https://mso-docs.github.io/mac-portfolio/).

## Research

**Unlearning vs. Hiding** (Lexsi AI Safety and Alignment Hackathon, Track 2b). Audits whether RMU unlearning erases or just hides facts in Tiny Aya (3.35B).
[Site](https://mso-docs.github.io/Lexsi-Unlearning/) · [Paper](https://mso-docs.github.io/Lexsi-Unlearning/downloads/Lexsi-Unlearning-paper.pdf) · [Code](https://github.com/mso-docs/Lexsi-Unlearning) · [Video](https://youtu.be/BuCfLNA-gfc)

**A Linear Answerability Signal That the Model Does Not Act On** (participant, Apart AI x Epistemics sprint). A linear probe finds a signal for answerability in Phi-3.5-mini's hidden states that the model's output does not follow, with probe AUROC 0.906 at layer 18 on SQuAD 2.0.
[Site](https://mso-docs.github.io/Apart-Epistemics/) · [Paper](https://mso-docs.github.io/Apart-Epistemics/downloads/Apart-Epistemics-paper.pdf) · [Code](https://github.com/mso-docs/Apart-Epistemics) · [Video](https://youtu.be/og3AKyBByQw)
